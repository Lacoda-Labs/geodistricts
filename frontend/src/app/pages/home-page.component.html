THE SOLUTION

How GeoDistricts Works

A simple, three-step process that any citizen can verify.

Start With Geography

Begin with the state's boundaries and census population data. No partisan data, no incumbent addresses — just people and places.

Recursive Lat-Long Splits

Alternately divide the state using latitude and longitude lines, splitting population as equally as possible. Each split creates two regions, then subdivide recursively.

Equal Population Districts

The result: compact, contiguous districts with near-equal population (within ~1% deviation). Transparent, reproducible, and free from partisan manipulation.

OUR PRINCIPLES
