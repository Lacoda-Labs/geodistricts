THE SOLUTION

How GeoDistricts Works

A simple, transparent, three-step algorithm that any citizen can verify and reproduce from public data.

1. Start With Neutral Geography

Use only official state boundaries and U.S. Census population data (tract or block level). No voter history, no party data, no incumbent addresses — just people and places.

2. Recursive Latitude & Longitude Splits

Alternately divide the state with straight north-south (longitude) and east-west (latitude) lines, balancing population as evenly as possible. For odd numbers of districts, splits can be uneven (e.g., 7 vs. 6). Lines snap to census tract boundaries for clean, practical divisions.

3. Equal, Compact Districts

The result: 435 contiguous, geographically compact districts with population deviation under ~1%. Deterministic, open-source, and reproducible by anyone.
