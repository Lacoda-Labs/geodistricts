**Key Insight:**
The algorithm only moves intersecting tracts (tracts that cross the dividing line), not all tracts. It stops when variance is balanced (typically around ±0.5-1.5%) to avoid overcorrection. The suggestion of ~104 tracts is an estimate, but the algorithm intelligently stops when variance is acceptable.
